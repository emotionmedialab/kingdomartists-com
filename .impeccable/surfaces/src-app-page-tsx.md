Mode: Persuade. Audience: Kingdom creatives (music-first) arriving from David & Cara's audience or a member's vouch link, mostly on phones. Job: understand what Kingdom Artists is, believe the Founding 300 is real and for them, and apply. Proof on hand: live accepted counter (/api/stats, counts accepted only — honesty rule), real application process (personal review, 24–48h, voice note), platform preview mockup, founder letter, character portraits in public/characters/. ## Direction contract

THESIS: Applying to Kingdom Artists is being booked for the session of your life, so the page IS the session sheet — one continuous studio booking document, set poster-grade. It refuses the category default this page previously shipped: the centered-hero startup waitlist with an app screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Paper white `#F7F6F2` ground ruled with cobalt form lines; exactly four inks — paper, cobalt `#1D44BE`, near-black `#101318`, stamp red `#D64541` (rare, one per view max). Blue-ruled field rows with typewritten-in values; grotesque caps for form labels (mono-spaced small caps), poster-scale grotesque for section titles, serif italic reserved for the wordmark's *kingdom* and handwritten-register moments (signatures, scripture). Components are form vocabulary: ruled fields, punched tabs, ink stamps (APPROVED/PENDING), signature lines, take counters, carbon-copy tint blocks. Every state is an ink event — stamped, punched, flagged, ruled — never a new hue. Recognizable with all content removed as a beautiful blank session sheet.

STORY: A creative lands on a session sheet already being filled out — SESSION: THE FOUNDING 300, their seat visibly open. They read the problem (isolation) as sheet notes, see the platform preview as the studio they're being booked into, understand the process as the sheet's routing stamps, hear the founders in a signed memo field, and sign: Apply = signing the sheet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed sheet header. Top rule: kingdomartists wordmark as letterhead left, sheet no. + live date right (mono labels). Beneath: giant two-line grotesque title "SESSION: / THE FOUNDING 300." with cobalt ruled underlines; a ruled field grid (DATE: NOW BOOKING · PERSONNEL: 300 SEATS · ENGINEER: A REAL PERSON · STATUS: <live counter> FILLED) with typed-in cobalt values; bottom-right, the signature field: a ruled line labeled SIGN HERE with the cobalt "Book My Seat →" button sitting ON the line. Mobile stacks the field grid two-up; title stays poster-scale.

FORM: Studio session/call-sheet world — candidate 3 of 7 on my ordered grounded list; seed key fb97c118. Signature interaction: the page fills itself in as you scroll — field values type in, stamps thunk down with a scale-settle, the take counter rolls, and the final Apply is a pen-stroke signature line draw. Motion grammar: ink events (draw, stamp, type) via motion/react, orchestrated once; no floating gradients, no parallax washes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
